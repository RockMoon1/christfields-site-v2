Iron and Ember is the name of the first FaithFlow group. It started small. It is still small. That is on purpose.

## How it started

A handful of us in Colorado realized we had drifted into a pattern that looks normal from the outside and is corrosive from the inside. We were busy. We were "in church." We had friends. But none of those friends were asking us the hard questions, and we were not asking them either.

So we started meeting. Not at a building. Not on a calendar app's recurring event. In someone's living room. With coffee.

The first meeting was awkward. The second was less awkward. The third we started actually telling the truth.

## What we are doing

We meet occasionally, not weekly. The cadence is decided by the people in the group, not by a brand calendar. Some weeks we meet twice. Some weeks once. Some weeks not at all.

Each meeting has a loose shape:

1. Open Scripture. We are working through John right now, slowly, one chapter at a time
2. Share where the week actually went, including the parts we would rather not share
3. Pray for each other by name
4. Eat something. Almost always

That is it. There is no curriculum binder. There is no points system. There is no app.

> "Carry each other's burdens, and in this way you will fulfill the law of Christ." — Galatians 6:2

## What it has cost

Time. The biggest cost is time. There is no version of real community that does not take time, and most people, including us, are very protective of their time.

Comfort. The second cost is comfort. Telling the truth about your week is not comfortable. Being told the truth about your week is not comfortable. We have leaned into both of those discomforts and they have been the most useful part.

Performance. The third cost has been giving up performance. Showing up tired. Showing up unprepared spiritually. Showing up after a hard week and not pretending it was fine. That has actually been a relief, not a loss.

## What we are learning

Faithfulness is a long pattern, not a moment.

You cannot fake your way through a group like this. The people next to you eventually know.

Scripture is heavier than we usually treat it. Reading it slowly, together, makes it bigger.

Praying for each other by name, every time, has changed the relationships in ways we did not predict.

## What is next

We are not currently expanding Iron and Ember to new members. The group works because the people in it have a history with each other. Adding people to that history requires care.

What may happen as Christ Fields grows is that NEW groups form. Different cities. Different names. Same principles. We are not in a hurry to make that happen.

If you read all of this and felt something pull, the form on /faithflow is where to send a real message. Not an application. A note. We respond personally.
