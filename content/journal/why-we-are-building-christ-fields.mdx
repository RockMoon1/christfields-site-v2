Christ Fields is not trying to be a tech company that happens to be Christian.
It is trying to be a Christian household that happens to build tools.

The difference is small. The difference is everything.

## What we are not

We are not building hype products. We are not chasing investors. We are not trying to grow fast.

If we built fast we would build cheap. If we built cheap we would build for performance, not for faithfulness. And we believe technology built for performance over faithfulness eventually exploits the people it claims to serve.

So we are slow on purpose. We are private on purpose. We are answering to a small list of names, in a small room, where Scripture is opened on a table.

## What we are

We are building tools and communities for people who want to live and work with wisdom, integrity, and faithfulness.

That means three things in practice.

> "As iron sharpens iron, so one person sharpens another." — Proverbs 27:17

**ScholarFlow** is for the work that happens behind closed doors. Time, distraction, discipline. The unseen hours.

**FaithFlow** is for the work that requires another human in the room. Accountability, friendship, study, prayer. The seen hours.

**OSINT & Trace** is for the work that exists to find people who have been lost. Service through technical skill. The hours nobody asked us to give.

These are not products. They are postures.

## A note on this journal

This is where we will write honestly about all of it. Build progress. Devotional reflections. What we are learning from Iron and Ember. Mistakes we have made.

We will not write to be impressive. We will write to be true.

If you signed up for the journey, this is the journey.
